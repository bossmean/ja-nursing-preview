Health Care Shield Membership

Membership terms

Plain language terms for the Health Care Shield Membership offered by JA Smith Consulting & Nursing Services.

Last updated November 2026.

1. Who we are

The Health Care Shield Membership is offered by JA Smith Consulting & Nursing Services, operated by Michelle Smith, RPN, CRTWC, in Ontario, Canada. In these terms, "we" and "us" mean JA Smith Consulting & Nursing Services, and "you" means the member.

2. What the membership includes

  • One 30 minute call with Michelle each month, booked through her online calendar.
  • Personalized preparation for your upcoming appointments, based on the information you share with Michelle.

Calls are booked one month at a time. A call that is not booked in a given month does not carry over to the next month.

3. What the membership is not

The membership is educational and organizational support. It does not replace medical advice, diagnosis or treatment from your own health care providers. It is not legal representation, and it is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call 911.

4. Price and billing

The membership costs $19.99 CAD per month. You are billed when you join and then automatically on the same day each month until you cancel. Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

If the price ever changes, we will tell you by email at least 30 days before the new price applies, so you have time to cancel if you wish.

5. Cancelling

You can cancel at any time using the Manage my membership link at the bottom of any page on this site. Enter the email you used to join and Stripe will send you a secure link. You can also email us at janursingservices@gmail.com and we will cancel it for you.

When you cancel, your membership stays active until the end of the month you have already paid for, and you will not be charged again.

6. Refunds

Monthly payments are not refunded once a billing month has started, because your membership remains active until the end of that month. If you believe you were charged in error, contact us and we will look into it.

7. Your information

How we collect, use and protect your information is explained in our Privacy Policy.

8. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. If a change affects your membership, we will let you know by email before it takes effect.

9. Contact

Questions about the membership or these terms? Email janursingservices@gmail.com or call 416 567 7556.
