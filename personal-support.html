What a health care advocate does

Someone in your corner to help you navigate what comes next.

A health care advocate helps individuals and families understand, navigate and organize their health care journey. The goal is not to make decisions for you. It is to help you understand your options, ask informed questions, reach the right resources and make decisions with more confidence.

Michelle brings experience as a Registered Practical Nurse, Certified Return to Work Coordinator and health and social advocate. This allows her to look beyond the immediate health concern and consider the factors that can affect a person's ability to navigate care, including family, work, income, housing and culture.

Advocacy support is educational and organizational. It does not replace medical advice, diagnosis or treatment from your own providers, it is not legal representation, and it is not an emergency service.

How Michelle can support you

Health care navigation

  • Understand health information, treatment plans and next steps

Disability and workplace navigation
