Free tools

Take the tool. Decide about the help later.

Each guide below is a real page from Michelle's practice, the same material she walks paying clients through. They are free, they print on one or two pages, and nobody will call you about them. Choose the one that matches the week you are having.

Not sure which one? Take the 2 minute score
Guide 01

The 7 Questions Kit

For anyone with an appointment coming up

Seven questions that change how an appointment goes, plus a one page sheet you fill in before you walk in and hand to whoever comes with you.

  • The seven questions, in the order to ask them
  • A printable pre appointment sheet
  • What to write down while you are still in the room
  • The one question most people forget on the way out
Guide 02

Hospital Discharge Checklist

For families bringing someone home

Eighteen things to confirm before a loved one leaves hospital, built from the gaps Michelle has watched send people straight back to emergency.

  • Medications, including what was stopped and why
  • Warning signs that mean call, and signs that mean 911
  • Home setup and safety before the car pulls up
  • Who is responsible for booking the follow up
Guide 03

Disability Claim Starter

For anyone off work or about to be

What insurers actually read, what your doctor needs from you before they fill out the form, and the record keeping that decides most appeals.

  • The three documents in every claim and who writes each one
  • How to describe function instead of feelings
  • A symptom and function log you can start today
  • What to do in the first week after a denial

Guide 04, the interactive one

The Health Care Readiness Score

Eight questions, about two minutes. It scores how prepared you actually are for your next appointment, hospital stay or claim, tells you which gap is costing you the most, and points you at the one thing to fix first.

Take the score

What you get at the end

  • A score out of 100 and where you sit
  • The specific gap to close first
  • Which of the free guides above matches it
  • An honest read on whether you need paid support at all

When the free pages run out

Each free guide covers one moment. The Health Care Shield Workbook covers the whole journey: family history, medications, appointment notes, care team contacts, blood pressure, emotional check ins, plain language explanations of specialists and tests, and future planning. Sixty nine pages you fill in once and carry from there.
