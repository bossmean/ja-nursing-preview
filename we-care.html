Changing the face of knowledge, one community at a time.

JA Nursing We Care Inc. is a community based non profit committed to improving health and social literacy through education, advocacy and community engagement. Founded in 2015.

Support this work Partner with us

Our mission

Knowledge is one of the most powerful tools for creating healthier communities.

We work alongside individuals, families, caregivers and community partners to reduce barriers to care, increase access to knowledge, and help people make informed decisions about their health and well being.

Through education, advocacy, community engagement and outreach, we help people gain the knowledge, confidence and resources needed to live healthier lives and build stronger communities.

Our programs

Four ways the work reaches people

Health literacy education

Helping people better understand health information and how health care systems actually work.

Community outreach

Bringing education and resources directly into the communities we serve.

Advocacy and awareness

Promoting the conversations that improve health and social outcomes.

Support for vulnerable populations

Reducing barriers to information, services and community resources.

Our approach

Health is influenced by more than health care alone.

Housing, education, employment, income, culture, community support and access to resources all shape well being. By addressing both health and the social determinants around it, we work to create meaningful and sustainable change.

Our approach is grounded in cultural competency, inclusion and respect, so that programs are responsive to the diverse needs of the communities we support.

  • Advancing equity, diversity and inclusion
  • Strengthening community capacity
  • Delivering meaningful and impactful programs
  • Promoting health literacy and self advocacy
  • Creating lasting positive change

Our social impact model

Two organizations, one mission.

JA Nursing We Care Inc. focuses on community outreach, education and advocacy. JA Smith Consulting and Nursing Services provides professional support to individuals, families, workplaces and organizations. A portion of proceeds from the consulting practice funds the non profit's work, which lets us reach further into the communities we serve.

Professional services fund community work

Advocacy clients, speaking engagements and workbook sales all contribute.

Community work reaches people who cannot pay

Workshops, outreach and health literacy education delivered where the barriers are highest.

The gap between education, advocacy and wellness narrows

Which is the whole point of building it this way.

Our impact

Since 2015

  • Promoting health and social literacy
  • Delivering community based education
  • Supporting individuals and families in making informed decisions
  • Increasing awareness of health and social issues
  • Providing outreach to underserved and vulnerable populations
  • Empowering community members to become advocates for themselves and others

Our continued growth is made possible through the support of community members, volunteers, partners and organizations who share the vision. We are available throughout the GTA and surrounding areas, as well as the West Indies.

Everything we do is guided by a simple belief: everyone deserves the right care and the right knowledge. As we grow, we remain committed to expanding our impact across Canada and throughout the African and Caribbean diaspora. Because we care.
