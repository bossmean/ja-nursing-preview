Support our work

Every contribution builds a more informed community.

JA Nursing We Care Inc. is a community based non profit established in 2015, dedicated to empowering people through health and social literacy. For over a decade we have worked alongside diverse communities to support informed decision making, strengthen well being, and build healthier, more resilient leaders.

How to donate

Why your support matters

Programs that address real community needs.

Your generosity lets us deliver programs that tackle the social determinants shaping health and social outcomes.

  • Improve access to health and social education
  • Empower individuals to make informed life choices
  • Support underserved communities
  • Create sustainable, community driven change

Our impact

Since 2015, JA Nursing We Care Inc. has stayed deeply rooted in the community, growing through the support of individuals and partners who believe in the mission. We continue to build on that foundation so the work remains community driven, accessible and useful.

Every contribution, whatever the size, helps create healthier individuals, stronger families and more informed communities.
