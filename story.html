Michelle's story

A journey of resilience, purpose and advocacy.

This is not a story defined by obstacles. It is a story of choosing to rise, rebuild, and advocate for others through some of life's hardest moments.

I believe everyone deserves the right care and the right knowledge, because lack of knowledge is the greatest disease.
Michelle Smith, JA Nursing

Where it all began

Born and raised in Toronto's west end, Michelle became a mother at seventeen. As a single mother, her community became her extended family, and it was through their support that her commitment to helping others began. She knew she wanted more, not only for her children Jessica and Anthony, but for herself and for the people around her. Nursing was more than a career choice. It was a calling to serve, educate and empower.

The redirection

In 1999, during her sixth semester of the RN Diploma program, a clinical setback led to her withdrawal. At the time it felt like everything she had worked for had come to an end. It was not the end. It was a redirection.

Redefining the path

Determined not to give up, Michelle drew on the knowledge and clinical training she had already gained across five semesters of RN education. Although she believed she had lost her chance to become a nurse, she chose to advocate for herself. With the support of her community, she was granted the opportunity to write the Registered Practical Nurse examination. In 2002 she became a Registered Practical Nurse.

Her foundation is built on RN level education, hands on clinical experience and lived resilience. That combination is what she brings to every individual and family she serves.

Purpose through loss

In 2002 Michelle lost her sister to cancer, and everything shifted. She began to see what many people do not talk about: the confusion, the silence, and the lack of guidance families face. It became clear that people are not just facing illness. They are navigating systems they do not fully understand.

People are not lacking services. They are lacking understanding. That realization became the foundation of her life's work.

Building something greater

In 2011 Michelle founded JA Smith Consulting and Nursing Services, named after her children Jessica and Anthony, who continue to inspire everything she does. The vision was never limited to health care alone. It was about empowering people through knowledge, helping them make informed decisions, access resources, and advocate for themselves and their families.

In 2015 that vision expanded through JA Nursing We Care Inc., a non profit dedicated to community wellness through education, advocacy, partnerships and outreach.

The wake up call

In 2017 Michelle became the patient. While working as a nurse at one of Toronto's largest hospitals, she became ill and found herself navigating the disability system from the other side. Despite her health care knowledge and professional experience, she was overwhelmed by complex paperwork, financial uncertainty, limited support and a system that was difficult to understand.

She experienced firsthand what so many people face when illness interrupts their ability to work. And she asked herself: if this can happen to me, a health care professional, what is happening to everyone else? What about the single parent trying to support a family? The worker living with chronic pain? The caregiver who spends every day helping others but receives little support when they need help themselves?

Expanding the advocacy

That experience transformed her mission. In 2019 Michelle earned her designation as a Certified Return to Work Coordinator through NIDMAR and Pacific Coast University. She immersed herself in disability management, occupational health, workers' compensation, workplace accommodation and return to work practice.

What she found was that many people are trying to navigate illness while also managing jobs, families, financial responsibilities and uncertain futures. Too often they feel alone, confused and unsupported. Advocacy, she realized, has to extend beyond health care.

Today she advocates not only for patients but for workers, parents, caregivers and people living with chronic illness who are trying to protect their health, keep their livelihood and preserve their dignity.

The mission

JA Nursing We Care Inc. is the community division of JA Smith Consulting and Nursing Services. Together, the mission is to promote community wellness while continuing to change the face of knowledge. Through education, advocacy and community engagement, they help people develop the awareness, confidence and understanding needed to navigate health care, disability and social support systems.

Known by many as the Voice of the Community, Michelle remains committed to empowering people of all ages with the knowledge, resources and support they need to reach their fullest potential.
