Why it exists

Why the Health Care Shield exists

Health information can quickly become difficult to keep track of. Appointments, medications, test results, referrals and follow up instructions may all be kept in different places.

The Health Care Shield gives you one place to organize important health information, keep it up to date and have it available when you need it.

How you use it

Before, during and after every appointment

Before you go

Write down what you want to ask while you can still think clearly, and check your medication list is current. You walk in with the questions already on paper instead of remembering them in the car park.

While you are there

Record what was actually said, what was ordered, and what happens next. When someone asks about your medications or history, turn to the page you need and share the details that matter, rather than trying to recall them under pressure.

After you leave

File the result, note the follow up, and update the record. The next appointment starts from what you already know instead of starting over.

What is inside

Eight sections you will actually use

Family health history

Keep important family health history organized in one place so it is easier to reference when needed.

Medication and supplement log

Every prescription, dose and over the counter product in one place, ready for the next pharmacy review.

Appointment prep and notes

Write the questions before you go, capture the answers while you are there, leave with a record.

Care team and emergency contacts

Who to call, in what order, when something goes wrong at an inconvenient hour.

Blood pressure tracker

Readings over time, so a pattern shows up instead of a single number on a single day.

Champion your feelings

A space to record how you are feeling and recognize how your health journey may be affecting you emotionally.

Plain language explanations

What specialists actually do, what common tests are for, and how referrals move through the system.

Future planning tools

Tools to help you think ahead and start important conversations about your wishes, Power of Attorney and protecting the people you care about.
