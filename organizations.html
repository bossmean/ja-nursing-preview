Why Michelle

Professional experience grounded in real world advocacy.

Michelle Smith is a Registered Practical Nurse, Certified Return to Work Coordinator, health and social advocate, author and community educator. With more than 24 years of nursing experience, she brings practical professional knowledge and lived experience to sessions designed to be informative, relatable and useful to real audiences.

  • Registered Practical Nurse, 24+ years
  • Certified Return to Work Coordinator through NIDMAR, 7+ years
  • Top 100 Canadian Black Women to Watch by CIBWE, 2015
  • Role Model and Achiever Award, Black Canadian Awards, 2016
  • Contributor to the Toronto Caribbean Newspaper, with previous health education experience on 105.5 FM
  • More than 314,000 page views across health education and community blog content
  • Author of The Health Care Shield Workbook

Signature topics

Built for real audiences

Each topic can be delivered as a keynote, workshop or facilitated session and adapted to the needs of your audience or event.

01Theme

Health care literacy, advocacy and community empowerment

Helping individuals, families and communities better understand and navigate health care through education, self advocacy, cultural awareness, inclusion and health equity.

02Theme

Workplace disability, accommodation and return to work

Practical education on occupational and non occupational disability management, workplace accommodation and return to work processes to support more informed and effective workplace practices.

03Theme

Mental wellness, resilience and entrepreneurship

Practical conversations about emotional wellness, stress, resilience and the pressures that can come with work, leadership and entrepreneurship.
