Why Michelle

Professional expertise, and the lived experience behind it.

Michelle Smith is an award winning health care advocate, Registered Practical Nurse, Certified Return to Work Coordinator, writer and community leader. Known as the Voice of the Community, she combines clinical expertise with lived experience to create presentations that are informative, relatable and genuinely useful.

  • Certified Return to Work Coordinator through NIDMAR, 7+ years
  • Registered Practical Nurse, 24+ years
  • Contributor to The Toronto Caribbean Newspaper
  • Founder of JA Smith Consulting and Nursing Services and JA Nursing We Care Inc.
  • Named one of the Top 100 Canadian Black Women to Watch by the CIBWE, 2015
  • Role Model and Achiever Award, Black Canadian Awards, Professional Role Model Excellence Conference, 2016
  • Over 160,000 readers reached through her educational blog and community content
  • Health care segment on 105.5 FM

Signature topics

Seven talks, built for real audiences

Each one can run as a keynote, a workshop or a facilitated session, and each is adapted to the room before it is delivered.

Health literacy and self advocacy

Helping people navigate health care systems, understand their options and advocate for better outcomes.

Mental health and wellness

Practical strategies for resilience, stress management, burnout prevention and emotional well being.

Workplace wellness and disability management

Healthier workplaces through prevention, accommodation, recovery and psychological safety.

Return to work and recovery

Supporting successful workplace reintegration through effective planning and recovery focused strategies.

Community empowerment

Building stronger communities through education, inclusion, cultural awareness and health equity.

Healthy aging and caregiver support

Practical tools for older adults and caregivers around wellness, safety and quality of life.

Youth leadership and life skills

Helping young people build confidence, leadership skills, resilience and healthy decision making.
